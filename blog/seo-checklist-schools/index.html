SEO Checklist for Schools and Education Centres

A practical SEO checklist for schools and education centres covering admissions pages, location details, parent questions and student privacy.

This guide is intended to help business owners make a useful next decision. Check platform settings and legal requirements at the time you implement a recommendation.

Make admissions information easy to find

Create a clear page for the current admissions process, required documents, contact route and dates that the school confirms. Keep expired notices out of the main journey and update details as dates change.

Explain programmes and facilities accurately

Parents may need to understand age groups, curriculum, transport, after-school activities or campus facilities. Each page should answer a specific question and avoid unverifiable rankings or promises about student outcomes.

Keep local signals consistent

Check the school's name, phone, website, location, hours and Google Business Profile. If there are multiple campuses, use the correct details for each one and do not create duplicate listings for the same location.

Use images responsibly

Campus photos can help families understand the environment, but student images need suitable permissions. Provide useful alt text that describes the image without naming children or exposing personal details.

Build helpful content around parent questions

Answer questions about visits, admissions, transport, activities and contacting the school. Avoid generating dozens of nearly identical locality pages that add no real information.

Measure visibility and enquiries

Review Search Console page and query data, profile interactions where available, and admissions enquiries the school can confirm. Keep dates and campaign context with the numbers so monthly comparisons remain fair.

How Hei Digi approaches this: start with the customer, keep business information accurate, measure actions in context and report what is known separately from what is assumed.
