Personal branding for doctors, founders and professionals

People choose a doctor, consultant or founder they trust. We turn your expertise into steady, useful posts, Reels and profile content so the right people know you before they meet you.

Discuss this serviceWhatsApp us

What is included

  • Positioning: who you help and what you want to be known for
  • Instagram and LinkedIn profile optimisation
  • Content pillars and monthly calendars
  • Reel scripts and educational posts
  • Personal brand creatives
  • Thought-leadership content in your voice

Who this service suits

Doctors, founders, consultants and creators with expertise worth sharing but little time to plan, write and design content.

How we work

  1. We talk through your experience, audience and goals.
  2. We build positioning and a content plan.
  3. We create content for your approval and review it monthly.

What this service looks like in Tirupati

Personal branding is not simply posting more photos. It is making a professional's point of view, expertise and personality clear enough that the right audience can remember them. A doctor may need patient-friendly education; a founder may need to explain decisions and lessons; a coach may need content that connects a useful idea to a clear next step.

Our approach

We interview the person, identify their audience and topics they can speak about credibly, and decide which platforms fit. Then we plan content pillars, hooks, profile positioning, scripts, filming guidance and a calendar. The person reviews factual claims and tone before publishing, and the plan develops as the audience responds.

What you receive

  • Profile and bio positioning
  • Audience and content-pillar map
  • Monthly calendar and Reel concepts
  • Scripts, captions and carousel outlines
  • Visual guidelines for a consistent profile
  • Editing support within agreed volume
  • Monthly review of available content metrics

How we measure progress

We may review follower count, reach, watch time, saves, shares, profile visits and inbound messages. Follower changes alone do not prove business value, and a short period does not guarantee repeatable growth. Where a case study is published, dates and metrics should match the source screenshots and the account owner's permission.

Timeline and approvals

The first month is used to establish direction and a repeatable production habit. A three-month period is useful for reviewing consistency and early signals, but results depend on the person appearing on camera, content quality, distribution and audience response.

Pricing and scope

Fees depend on how much strategy, scripting, creative production, editing, publishing and community management is included. Filming days, travel, paid promotion and extra revisions can be quoted separately. We agree the person's time commitment before starting.

What to prepare before kickoff

Share the person's background, topics they can discuss accurately, professional rules that apply, existing content, preferred language, and any privacy boundaries. For healthcare or education, approvals and consent are especially important; do not publish patient or child material without valid permission.

What we do not promise

For healthcare professionals we keep content educational and avoid exaggerated claims. Please confirm it meets the advertising rules of your medical council. You approve every post before it goes live.

Questions about Personal Branding

Do you need my photos and videos?

Real photos and videos build trust. We can guide you on what to capture.

Will the content sound like me?

We write from your interview and your own explanations, and you approve it before posting.

Can you manage LinkedIn too?

Yes. We plan LinkedIn content alongside Instagram.

Can you guarantee follower growth?

No. We can build positioning and a steady content system, then review actual account results. Audience growth is not guaranteed.

Does personal branding include Reels?

It can. The quantity and production style are stated in the monthly scope.

Can you manage Instagram and LinkedIn together?

Yes, if both platforms suit the audience and the content is adapted for each one.

Will content be written in my voice?

We use interviews and your feedback so the content reflects what you actually believe and can explain.
