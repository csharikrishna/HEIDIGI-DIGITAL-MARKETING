Content strategy for consistent marketing

Without a plan, content turns into guesswork. We decide what to say, to whom and when, then turn it into a calendar that your team or ours can follow.

Discuss this serviceWhatsApp us

What is included

  • Content pillars based on your goals and audience
  • Monthly content calendars
  • Campaign and festival ideas
  • Scripts and storytelling angles
  • A balance of educational and promotional content
  • A simple monthly review

Who this service suits

Teams that post but without direction, and businesses preparing a launch or a seasonal campaign.

How we work

  1. We review your goals, audience and existing content.
  2. We define the pillars and a calendar.
  3. We hand over the plan or produce the content with you.

What this service looks like in Tirupati

A content calendar has value only when it connects a business goal to topics the audience cares about and a production routine the team can sustain. A school may need admissions answers and campus updates, while a local manufacturer may need product explainers, delivery information and proof of capability. A useful plan is built around those real questions, not just festival dates.

Our approach

We review the audience, offer, existing channels and approval process, then group topics into pillars. The calendar balances educational, trust-building, product or service and promotional content. Each item gets an intended format, owner, date and call to action so the client can see what is being made and why.

What you receive

  • Audience and message review
  • Content pillars and recurring themes
  • Monthly calendar with formats and owners
  • Campaign ideas linked to offers and dates
  • Briefs, hooks and script outlines
  • Approval and asset collection workflow
  • Monthly review and adjustments

How we measure progress

We evaluate whether agreed content was published, whether the audience consumed or interacted with it, and whether it helped create relevant visits or enquiries where tracking is available. A calendar itself is a planning output, not proof of business growth.

Timeline and approvals

The first plan normally starts with an interview and content audit. Calendar production gets easier when the client provides assets and approvals by a consistent deadline. Seasonal and launch plans should begin early enough to allow design, review and publishing.

Pricing and scope

The fee depends on the number of channels, content volume, whether scripts and creatives are included, research depth, and whether publishing or reporting is also managed. A strategy-only engagement costs differently from a full production retainer.

What to prepare before kickoff

Bring your services and prices, target audience, seasonal dates, past posts, current offers, approved assets and the person responsible for sign-off. Tell us what cannot be claimed or shown and what business outcome each campaign should support.

What we do not promise

A plan only helps if it is followed, so we set a realistic pace for your team.

Questions about Content Strategy

Is this different from social media management?

Strategy is the plan. Management is the day-to-day work. You can take either or both.

Can my own team use the plan?

Yes. We can hand over the calendar and scripts for your team to execute.

Do you plan festival campaigns?

Yes. Festival and seasonal dates are built into the calendar.

Is content strategy the same as social media management?

No. Strategy defines what and why to publish; management can include creating, scheduling and responding to content.

Can our team execute the calendar?

Yes. We can deliver a plan and briefs for your own team to use.

Do you plan festival content?

Yes, where it is appropriate for your audience and brand, alongside evergreen content.

Can it include SEO articles?

Yes. Search topics can be included, with separate briefs and publishing scope for articles.
