Video editing and AI videos for business marketing

Short videos get a message across faster than a static post. We edit the footage you have, build motion graphics, and use AI tools where they help, with a person checking every result.

Discuss this serviceWhatsApp us

What is included

  • Instagram Reels and short-form editing
  • Promotional and announcement videos
  • Captions, titles and motion graphics
  • AI-assisted video concepts and scenes
  • Scripts and storyboards
  • Exports for Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and WhatsApp

Who this service suits

Businesses with footage but no editor, and brands that want fresh visual ideas without a full shoot.

How we work

  1. We agree the idea, length and platform.
  2. We edit or generate the visuals and add captions and sound.
  3. We share a draft, apply feedback and export the final.

What this service looks like in Tirupati

A short video should make its point in the first few seconds and still make sense with the sound off. For a local business, that may mean a service demonstration, an admissions update, a product walk-through or a founder explaining one useful idea. AI can support ideation and production, but every output needs human review and honest disclosure when appropriate.

Our approach

We agree the audience, message, platform, length and footage before editing. The edit may include a clear hook, captions, clean cuts, supporting visuals, logo placement and a next step. AI-assisted scenes are used where they suit the brief; a real demonstration, licensed footage or client-provided material is better when trust depends on the real product or person.

What you receive

  • Instagram Reels and short-form edits
  • Promotional and announcement videos
  • Captions and on-screen text
  • AI-assisted concept frames or scenes
  • Scripts, hooks and storyboard guidance
  • Aspect-ratio exports for selected platforms
  • Final render and agreed revision round

How we measure progress

When platform data is available, reports may include video views, average watch time, completion, interactions, saves, profile visits and enquiries. View definitions differ by platform, so the report records the source and does not treat all views as equivalent.

Timeline and approvals

Timing depends on source-footage quality, video duration, subtitle language, music rights, approvals and the number of revisions. A clear brief and one consolidated round of feedback usually speed up delivery.

Pricing and scope

Pricing depends on video length, editing complexity, motion graphics, AI generation, voiceover, subtitle languages and the amount of footage. Licensing or studio filming may cost extra. We specify the number of deliverables instead of offering unlimited edits.

What to prepare before kickoff

Share the footage you own or are authorised to use, the brand logo, correct names and claims, preferred references, target length and publishing date. Obtain permission from people appearing in the video, especially children and patients, and confirm any music or third-party material is licensed.

What we do not promise

We label AI-generated visuals where it matters, and we do not use a real person's face or voice without permission.

Questions about Video Editing & AI Videos

Do you shoot videos?

This service covers editing and AI-assisted production from footage and material you provide.

Will AI videos look fake?

It depends on the subject. We tell you what suits AI and what is better filmed.

How long does a Reel take?

Timelines are confirmed in the quote because they depend on the footage and the number of revisions.

Will AI videos always look realistic?

No. Some ideas work well with AI while real people, products or locations may need real footage. We will flag limitations before production.

Can you edit footage I already have?

Yes. We can work from client-supplied footage if it is clear enough and you have rights to use it.

Do you provide unlimited revisions?

No. The quote includes a defined revision scope to keep timing and expectations clear.

Can you add Telugu subtitles?

Yes, with client confirmation of wording and spelling before final export.
