Frequently Asked Questions

What does SEO include at Hei Digi?

SEO work can include technical checks, keyword and search-intent mapping, page titles and headings, useful service content, internal links, local business information and Search Console review when access is available.

Can you guarantee a top Google ranking?

No. Google controls search and Maps rankings. We focus on accurate information, useful content, technical quality and transparent reporting, without promising a position.

How do I contact Hei Digi quickly?

Call +91 97055 70007 or use the WhatsApp button to start a chat. You can also get directions to the Tirupati office from the contact section.

Where is Hei Digi based?

Hei Digi is a digital marketing agency based in Tirupati, Andhra Pradesh, India.

Which services do you offer?

We offer search engine optimization (SEO), Google Business Profile management, social media marketing, Meta Ads, creative design, video editing and AI videos, branding, personal branding and content strategy.

Which industries have you worked with?

Our client list includes businesses in healthcare, education, construction, steel and manufacturing, and hospitality.

What does a monthly performance report cover?

Where we have account access, reporting can cover work delivered, Google Search Console visibility, Google Business Profile interactions, social and ad performance, spend, enquiries and next actions. We separate platform metrics from confirmed sales.

Is ad spend included in your fee?

No. Ad spend is paid directly to Meta from your ad account. Our service fee is separate.

Do you use AI?

Yes, for ideas, drafts, research and some visuals. People review and edit the work, and we label AI visuals where it matters.

Do you work with doctors and professionals?

Yes. We build educational personal-brand content for doctors, founders and consultants, and you approve every post.

How do I get started?

Send an enquiry from the contact form, call us or message us on WhatsApp. We ask about your goals first. Pricing depends on scope, so we share a quote after that conversation.
